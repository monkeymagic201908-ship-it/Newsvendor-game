You just experienced the Newsvendor Problem.

The challenge is not predicting demand perfectly. It is choosing an order quantity that balances the consequences of ordering too much and too little.

📉 Overage cost
Co = $12 − $6 = $6
Cost associated with one leftover shirt.
📈 Underage cost
Cu = $30 − $12 = $18
Contribution margin lost from one missed sale.
CRITICAL RATIO
18 ÷ (18 + 6) = 0.75

Target the 75th percentile of demand — not 75% of average demand.

≈ 554 shirts
